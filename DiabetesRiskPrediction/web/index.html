Important Scientific Distinction: In ensemble Random Forest architectures, feature importance quantifies how heavily the decision trees rely upon a specific attribute when partitioning feature space and minimizing out-of-bag classification loss.

A high Mean Decrease in Accuracy (such as observed in HbA1c_level and blood_glucose_level) establishes that permuting these variables significantly degrades diagnostic prediction power within this dataset. This metric demonstrates statistical model reliance, and must not be conflated with direct biological causation.
